Student Pulse

A student board for sharing how school and events are going. ← Back to the board

Board rules

  1. Be honest, be kind.
  2. Criticize ideas and policies, not people.
  3. No names in complaints.
  4. Tag spoilers and sensitive stuff.

Anonymous posts and comments

You can post or comment anonymously. Other students cannot see who wrote an anonymous post or comment, and you can still edit or delete your own anonymous posts and comments.

Moderators cannot see who wrote an anonymous post or comment either, with one exception: if it is reported for bullying, harassment or a threat to safety, a moderator may reveal the author so the school can act. Every reveal is recorded in the moderator activity log. Any post or comment can be reported with the Report button.

Moderation

Moderators can pin, lock or remove posts, warn students and ban accounts that break the rules. Moderators write down a reason when they ban someone, and other moderators can see it. A word filter stops posts, comments and profiles that contain blocked words or phrases. If you think a decision was wrong, speak to a moderator.

Your data

Your account, posts and photos are kept on this site. You can edit or delete your own posts and comments at any time (edited posts and comments are marked “edited”), and you can delete your account from your profile page. When an account is deleted, its old posts and comments stay on the board as “Anonymous”.

Who can use it

This board is for students of our school. Use it only as yourself, do not share other people's private information, and do not post anything harmful or illegal.
